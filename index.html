Civic Terrace · Meridian City · After the rain

A living corner of a city that doesn't exist: wet stone, low sun, a broadcast that never ends, and people with nowhere urgent to be.

Click the stone and the figure in amber will walk there. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, let it rain if you miss the sound.

Everything here is procedural — stone, puddles, rain, light and sound are generated in your browser with three.js. No footage, no photos, no models.
